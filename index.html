Our Covenant Mandate

Come and Experience A Place Of

Rooted in God’s promise in Genesis 47:27, Goshen Christian Centre is a spiritual home where every believer steps into supernatural fruitfulness and divine peace.

#Multiplication

Supernatural Multiplication

Just as Israel multiplied exceedingly in Goshen, we believe God multiplies your spiritual gifts, family blessings, and Kingdom impact beyond natural limits.

Lives Transformed Genesis 47:27
#Increase

All-Round Increase

Through the uncompromising teaching of the Word and fervent prayer, faith is strengthened so you grow in wisdom, grace, and good possession in every season.

Faith Strengthened Proverbs 4:18
#Rest

Divine Rest & Peace

In the midst of a busy world, Goshen is a sanctuary of divine protection, healing, and spiritual rest where God-given destinies are nurtured and fulfilled.

Destinies Fulfilled Matthew 11:28
